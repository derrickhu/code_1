村民三阶进化立绘 · 批量规格
========================

用途：图鉴 / 详情页大图。战场打击仍走现有 anim_{id}_idle_0-3 + anim_{id}_atk_0-3 八帧 sprite。
一阶以 assets/images/hero_{id}.png 为脸和身型基准。

公共风格（每张必带）
--------------------
One horizontal row, 3 equal panels, plain flat white background.
SAME person in all 3: chunky Chinese rural cartoon, big head, thick black outlines,
flat cel shading TWO shadow steps only, palette: earth yellow / cement grey / plastic blue / rusty red.
Full body, front 3/4, feet on ground, same eye level and camera.
Use the reference image for FACE, body proportions, and line weight only.

SILHOUETTE LAW:
Panel 2 must be clearly WIDER than panel 1 (shoulder / prop sticks out).
Panel 3 must be TALLER and bulkier than panel 2 (thing on head AND thing on back).
If filled solid black, a stranger ranks them 1 < 2 < 3 instantly.
Do NOT just recolor or add tiny badges.

NO TEXT, no labels, no numbers, no captions, no frames.
