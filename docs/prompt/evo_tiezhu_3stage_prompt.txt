铁柱三阶进化立绘（剪影测试版）
================================

用途：验证「进化必须改剪影」的美术规格。三张并排，同一个人，轮廓一眼分得出。
参考图：assets/images/hero_tiezhu.png（一阶，风格与脸型基准）

--- PROMPT ---

One horizontal row, 3 panels, equal width, plain flat white background.
The SAME chunky cartoon character in all 3 panels: a stocky Chinese rural young man,
big head, thick black outlines, flat cel shading with only two shadow steps,
limited palette of warm earth yellow, cement grey, plastic blue, rusty red.
Same face in all three: thick angry eyebrows, small determined eyes, no smile.
Full body, front-facing, standing, feet on ground, same eye level across all 3 panels.

PANEL 1 (left) - baseline, copy the reference image exactly:
Red and blue and olive-green layered puffer jacket, three visible horizontal quilted bands.
Dark red motorcycle helmet with grey visor brim. Blue padded trousers, blue boots.
Empty hands hanging at sides. Compact narrow silhouette, shoulders normal width.

PANEL 2 (middle) - welded steel plates, MUST look wider and bulkier:
Same puffer jacket underneath, but now crude rusty steel plates are bolted onto the chest,
both shoulders and both forearms, with visible rivets and rough weld seams.
Shoulder plates stick out sideways beyond the body, making the silhouette clearly WIDER.
The helmet now has a flipped-up welding mask attached on top, breaking the head outline upward.
He holds a heavy rusted pipe wrench in his right hand, resting on his shoulder.
Overall color shifts greyer and more metallic than panel 1.

PANEL 3 (right) - pressure cooker head and mattress backpack, MUST be the biggest and tallest:
The motorcycle helmet is GONE, replaced by a big battered aluminium pressure cooker worn
upside down as a helmet, its handle and pressure valve sticking up above the head.
A thick striped spring mattress is strapped vertically to his back like a shield,
clearly taller and wider than his body, sticking out well beyond both shoulders and above.
More steel plates, chains and rope wrapped around torso and legs.
He holds a big round pot lid as a shield in the left hand and the pipe wrench in the right.
Rusty red and metallic highlights, the most cluttered and heaviest of the three.

CRITICAL SILHOUETTE REQUIREMENT:
If all three figures were filled in solid black as pure silhouettes, a stranger must
instantly tell them apart and rank them from weakest to strongest.
Panel 2 must be noticeably wider than panel 1.
Panel 3 must be noticeably taller and bulkier than panel 2, with things sticking out
above the head and behind the back.
Do NOT just add small badges or recolor - the outline itself must change.

NO TEXT, no labels, no captions, no numbers, no writing anywhere in the image.
No panel borders, no frames, no drop shadows on the background.
