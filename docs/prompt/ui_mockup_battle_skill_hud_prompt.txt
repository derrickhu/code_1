Mobile game BATTLE HUD mockup, vertical 9:16 WeChat minigame, lane tower defense "村口大战外星人".

ART STYLE: same as reference — rustic Chinese village scrap-yard, sunset dirt road, cheap sticker-chibi, fat comic ink,
cream #FFF4C4, gold #C9A46A, ink #2A160C, rusty riveted iron plates. 土味 real objects, no sci-fi HUD.
FORBIDDEN: purple glow panels, neon, English words, phone frame, watermark.

BATTLEFIELD: dirt road split into 3 vertical lanes, villagers standing in the lower part (2 rows, about 6 villagers:
old man with slingshot, man with sledgehammer, auntie with fishing net, fat guy in padded coat, old man with tin megaphone, uncle with wrench).
Aliens (grey big-head aliens, tin-can robot, saucer) come DOWN from the top as a CROWD — a dense rush group in the middle lane
pushing close to the front villager, some hitting him (impact stars). Feels tense, not single file.
One BIG alien boss (fat tin-can robot, 2x size) walking in the left lane with its own long red HP bar above head and label 大个子.

TOP rusty iron bar: left "3-4 晒场"; middle 3 wave pips with 2 filled, caption 第3波; right fence icon with 漏 1/3 in red.

TOP-RIGHT under the bar, two small round iron buttons stacked: one reads ×2 (speed, lit gold), one reads 自动 (toggle, lit green light).

BOTTOM: a rusty iron skill tray with 3 round portrait buttons of villagers whose ultimate is READY (glowing gold ring, slight bounce):
slingshot old man, fishing-net auntie, megaphone old man. Under each a tiny cream label: 滑轮连射 / 拦河大网 / 全村广播.
Next to them 2 dim empty sockets. Above each villager on the field a thin yellow 劲头 bar (energy), some full, some half.

Readable at arm length; the skill tray and speed button should be obvious.
