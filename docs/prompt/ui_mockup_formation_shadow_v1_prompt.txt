INDUSTRY RULE (Tata / TFT / Arknights):
The dirt road IS the board. Markings are almost invisible until you look for them. Never cover the ground with UI panels.

BATTLEFIELD (middle 55% of frame):
- Same village dirt alley: cracked ochre earth, blue brick, washing machine, crates, sandbags at the near edge.
- High almost-top-down, path stays wide (about 70% width), barely tapers.
- THREE lanes implied only by faint worn wagon-ruts / packed-dust tracks in the dirt. Same color as the ground, just slightly darker dust. NO yellow, NO brown glass, NO tinted rectangles.
- Lower half of the road: a 3-column × 4-row deploy zone.
- Empty slots: hairline dashed squares scratched into the dirt, like a stick dragged on dry earth. Extremely light. You can still see every crack and pebble through them.
- Occupied slots: ONLY a small soft contact shadow under the character's feet (tiny oval, 30% of a cell, like late-afternoon sun). No pad, no platform, no big stamp.
- One selected unit: a thin gold ring drawn ON the dirt around the feet, small, not a glow slab.
- Upper road (enemy approach): empty dirt, 4–5 tiny grey alien silhouettes far away. No grid there.

UNITS:
- Three village loafers as chess pieces, about 1/3 of a lane wide. Feet planted on dirt.
- 王大锤 (hammer, overalls), 铁柱 (helmet, padded jacket), 弹弓叔 (slingshot). Small dark name tags at feet.

UI (keep village rust, do not invent candy UI):
