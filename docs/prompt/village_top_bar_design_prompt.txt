Mobile game TOP HUD design mockup.

The entire TOP of the frame is ONE full-bleed rusted-iron lintel.
It must touch the left edge, the right edge, and the top edge.
ZERO gaps. No sky, no clouds, no empty corners beside or above the metal.
This is a welded village-gate header, not a floating centered plaque.

Inside the lintel, stacked and riveted as one piece:
- Large brush-painted gold-yellow Chinese 村子 1级 on the metal
- A recessed rusted exp slot with a short orange fill
- Small painted caption 再 112 点 · 多上一人
- Four welded stamp pockets in one row: 废铁 250 / 零件 0 / 工分 0 / 弹子 20
  with scrap / gear / medal / pellet icons

Cheap-fun Chinese small-town sticker look. Fat comic ink outlines, two-tone cel shade.
Painted flaky orange rust, dark umber iron, heavy rivets, bolted seams.
Same rust material as the attached plates and home mockup.

Below the lintel, a dusk village dirt road continues so the header sits flush on the scene.

No WeChat buttons, no phone notch, no English, no logos, no watermarks,
no white boards, no clean plastic cartoon UI.
