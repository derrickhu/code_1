保温壶爷三阶立绘样例（规范 v2）
参考图只用于脸和格子衫，不要抄参考图的裁切和构图。

--- PROMPT ---

One horizontal row, 3 equal panels, plain flat white background, no panel borders.
The SAME chunky Chinese rural grandpa in all 3 panels: balding shiny scalp, grey hair on the sides,
thick grey eyebrows, half-lidded gentle eyes, small warm smile, light wrinkles.
Red-and-blue plaid button shirt, brown trousers, white rope belt bow, brown cloth slippers.
Big head, thick black comic outlines, flat cel shading TWO shadow steps only.
Palette: earth yellow, cement grey, plastic blue, rusty red. No texture noise.
Full body, front 3/4 view, feet planted, same eye level and camera in every panel.

SAFE FRAME IN EVERY PANEL:
The entire figure AND every prop must sit inside the panel with about 12 percent empty white
margin on all four sides. Headgear not touching the top. Feet not touching the bottom.
No bucket, pipe, kettle, valve, or shoe clipped by the edge.
Horizontal props must stay under 70 percent of the panel width. Pull wide gear inward.

PANEL 1 LEFT — evo1, weakest, narrowest, everyday:
He holds ONE household stainless thermos with a red screw-cap in both hands, close to his chest.
No helmet, no backpack, no extra tanks. Compact silhouette, empty sides.

PANEL 2 MIDDLE — evo2, clearly WIDER:
He holds a much larger dented dark-iron kettle with a fat spout, both hands, steam puff from the spout.
A tall stainless thermos is strapped to his BACK with rope, sticking out behind the shoulders.
Same shirt. No helmet yet. Silhouette wider than panel 1 because of the back thermos and bigger kettle.

PANEL 3 RIGHT — evo3, strongest, tallest, MUST look armed for a fight, NOT a peaceful chore:
Same grandpa face, but now scrap-combat ready.
HEAD: a battered red construction / motorcycle helmet with a short visor, chin strap on.
BACK / SHOULDERS: a SHORT scrap-metal carrying frame hugged CLOSE to his body (not a long village yoke).
Two fat industrial pressure tanks / hot-water drums sit at his hips, valves and steam pipes sticking
UP above the shoulders, making him taller. Small steam puffs from the valves.
RIGHT HAND: a long rusted steel PIPE wrench-club resting on his shoulder, fully visible, not cropped.
Rope, rivets, rust stains on the tanks. He looks like he can walk onto a battlefield and speed the team,
NOT like he is just fetching water from the well.
Silhouette is the widest and tallest of the three.

SILHOUETTE LAW:
If filled solid black, a stranger ranks them 1 < 2 < 3 instantly.
Do not just recolor. Do not use a long wooden shoulder pole that sticks out of the frame.

NO TEXT, no labels, no numbers, no captions, no frames, no ground shadow.
